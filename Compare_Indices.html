For the layman investor — the simplest guide you'll ever need:

  • Nifty Total Market is the ultimate "one fund" — it captures the entire Indian market (all listed companies) with a smooth 14.12% CAGR and only 2 negative years. Track it via AONETOTAL ETF. 🌟
  • Nifty 500 is the practical "one fund" — it captures ~95% of the market with almost identical performance (13.83% CAGR) and even lower volatility (-1.55% worst year). 🌍
  • Midcap 150 gives the best returns (17.47% CAGR) with only 1 negative year — the "sweet spot" for growth seekers. 🏆
  • Nifty 50 is the safest — only 1 negative year, smallest drawdown (-14.3%). 🛡️

  • Nifty Next 50 is the steady middle child — matches Nifty 500 returns with moderate risk. ⚖️
7. What's Inside Nifty Total Market?

Nifty Total Market includes all companies listed on the NSE. It is the sum of:

  • ✔ Nifty 500 (top 500 companies) — ~95% of market cap
  • ✔ Remaining listed companies (beyond top 500) — ~3-5% of market cap
Key insight: Nifty Total Market gives you every single company — no one is left out. This is the most comprehensive Indian equity index available.
